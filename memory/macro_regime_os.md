## STEP 1: Global Liquidity Analysis

### คำถามที่ต้องตอบ
> "เงินในระบบเยอะขึ้นหรือน้อยลง? และมันกำลังไปทางไหน?"

### Indicators & Framework
```
A) REAL RATES (สำคัญที่สุด)
   - Nominal 10Y yield - Breakeven Inflation = Real Rate
   - Real Rate ลดลง → Liquidity เพิ่ม → Risk assets ดีขึ้น
   - Real Rate เพิ่มขึ้น → Liquidity ลด → Risk assets กดดัน
   - Threshold: Real rate > 2% = ตึงมาก; < 0% = ผ่อนคลายมาก

B) CENTRAL BANK BALANCE SHEETS
   - Fed + ECB + BoJ + PBoC = Global Liquidity Proxy
   - ขยาย (QE) → สัปดาห์ต่อมา Risk assets tend to rally
   - หดตัว (QT) → Headwind for all risk assets
   - Michael Howell: Global CB balance sheet เดิน cycle 5-7 ปี

C) FINANCIAL CONDITIONS INDEX (FCI)
   - Goldman Sachs FCI หรือ Bloomberg FCI
   - หดตัว (tightening) → ระวัง lag effect 6-12 เดือน
   - ผ่อนคลาย (easing) → Support ใน 3-6 เดือน

D) US DOLLAR (DXY) — Liquidity Gauge
   - USD แข็ง → Global liquidity หด (dollar ดึงกลับ) → EM แย่
   - USD อ่อน → Global liquidity เพิ่ม → EM/Commodity ดี
   - "Dollar smiles" theory: แข็งในช่วง panic หรือ US outperformance

E) TGA / REVERSE REPO (US Treasury)
   - TGA (Treasury General Account) ลด → เงินปล่อยลงระบบ
   - Reverse Repo (RRP) ลด → เงินออกจาก safe หา yield
   - ทั้งสองตัวนี้ = hidden liquidity indicator
```

### Liquidity Scorecard
```
Score 1-5:
5 = Very Easy (Real rate < 0, CB expanding, FCI loose, USD weak)
4 = Easy
3 = Neutral
2 = Tight
1 = Very Tight (Real rate > 2%, CB shrinking, FCI tight, USD strong)
```

### คำถามที่ต้องตอบ
> "เศรษฐกิจอยู่จุดไหนของวัฏจักร? กำลังเร็วขึ้นหรือช้าลง?"

### First Derivative Framework — Rate of Change สำคัญกว่า Level
```
PMI 48 rising from 44 → BULLISH (momentum turning up)
PMI 54 falling from 58 → BEARISH (deceleration starting)
PMI 50.5 for 6 months straight → NEUTRAL (stagnation)

อย่าดูแค่ว่า PMI > 50 — ดูว่ามันกำลังขึ้นหรือลง
```

### Key Indicators
```
A) ISM MANUFACTURING PMI
   - > 50 = Expansion; < 50 = Contraction
   - New Orders Sub-index: Leading indicator (2-3 months lead)
   - Prices Paid Sub-index: Inflation pressure indicator
   - Employment Sub-index: Labor market signal

B) ISM SERVICES PMI
   - US = 70% services economy → มีน้ำหนักมาก
   - Business Activity component: Real growth signal
   - New Orders: Forward momentum

C) EMPLOYMENT
   - Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP): Lagging indicator
   - Initial Claims (Weekly): Leading indicator (turning point faster)
   - JOLTS (Job Openings): Leading → ลดลง = labor market softening
   - Wage Growth: Inflation → Fed policy input

D) CREDIT GROWTH (Loan Growth)
   - Commercial & Industrial Loans: Business investment indicator
   - Consumer Credit: ถ้า tight = consumption slowing
   - Senior Loan Officer Survey (SLOOS): Lending standards tightening?

E) RETAIL SALES & CONSUMPTION
   - Core Retail Sales (ex-auto, gas): Real consumer demand
   - Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE): Fed-preferred
```

### Economic Cycle Position
```
ACCELERATING:  PMI rising, New Orders strong, Claims falling, Wages growing
PEAK:          PMI high but flattening, New Orders peak, Claims bottoming
DECELERATING:  PMI falling, New Orders weak, Claims rising
TROUGH:        PMI near 44-46, Claims spike, Credit tight, ISM < 48
```

## STEP 3: Inflation Regime Analysis

### คำถามที่ต้องตอบ
> "Inflation กำลังขึ้น ลง หรือนิ่ง? และมาจากไหน?"

### Inflation Decomposition (อย่าดู CPI แค่ตัวเดียว)
```
CPI COMPONENTS ที่สำคัญ:
│
├── GOODS INFLATION (สินค้า)
│   ├── New Cars / Used Cars — volatile, supply chain indicator
│   ├── Apparel — global trade indicator
│   └── Electronics — technology deflation offset
│
├── SERVICES INFLATION (บริการ) — stickier, harder to bring down
│   ├── Shelter (OER) — 33% of CPI, lags real rent by 12-18 months
│   ├── Medical Services
│   └── Transportation services
│
└── SUPERCORE (Services ex-Housing ex-Energy)
    = Fed's most watched (reflects wage-driven inflation)
    ถ้า supercore ไม่ตก = Fed ยังกังวล
```

### Inflation Regime Classification
```
DEFLATIONARY:    CPI < 1%, falling
DISINFLATION:    CPI falling but still positive (good for stocks)
STABLE:          CPI 1.5-2.5%, stable (Goldilocks)
REFLATION:       CPI 2.5-4%, rising (good early, bad late)
INFLATION:       CPI 4-6%, Fed reacting
STAGFLATION:     CPI > 4% + GDP slowing (worst combo)
HYPERINFLA:      CPI > 8% (breakdown scenario)
```

### Key Signal: Breakeven Inflation Rate
```
5Y5Y Forward Breakeven (FRED: T5YIFR)
- > 2.5% and rising = market pricing in higher inflation ahead
- < 2.0% = market fears deflation
- Falling from high = disinflation trade beginning
```

## STEP 4: Credit Conditions Analysis

### คำถามที่ต้องตอบ
> "ตลาดสินเชื่อ stress ไหม? Credit เป็น canary ใน coal mine"

### Credit Hierarchy (เรียงตามความสำคัญของสัญญาณ)
```
1. HY (High Yield) SPREAD — ตัวแรกที่เปลี่ยน ก่อน equity จะตก
   FRED: BAMLH0A0HYM2
   < 3%  = Euphoria / Risk-on maximum
   3-4%  = Calm, normal credit conditions
   4-5%  = Caution, credit slightly stressed
   5-7%  = Stress, avoid high-risk positions
   > 7%  = Crisis, move to safety
   > 10% = Systemic risk (2008 = 20%+)

2. IG (Investment Grade) SPREAD
   FRED: BAMLC0A0CM
   IG widens before HY in early stress
   IG spread > 150bp = starting to price recession

3. LEVERAGED LOAN SPREAD
   Leveraged loans = floating rate → sensitive to rate path
   ถ้า spread กว้างขึ้น + refinancing risk = PE/LBO stress

4. CDS (Credit Default Swaps)
   CDX IG / CDX HY indices
   Spike = credit market panicking

5. SENIOR LOAN OFFICER SURVEY (SLOOS)
   Fed publishes quarterly
   "Net % tightening lending standards"
   > 20% tightening = recession signal historically
   This is THE leading indicator — 6-9 months ahead

6. DELINQUENCY RATES
   Credit card, auto, commercial real estate delinquencies
   Rising = consumer/business stress
   Most useful as CONFIRMATION of stress that started in HY
```

### Credit Cycle Position
```
EASY:     HY < 3%, IG < 100bp, SLOOS loose, delinquencies low
NORMAL:   HY 3-4%, SLOOS neutral
TIGHTENING: HY 4-5%, SLOOS net tightening > 10%, delinquencies rising
STRESSED: HY 5-7%, credit crunch beginning
CRISIS:   HY > 7%, lending stops, liquidity evaporates
```

## STEP 5: Market Internals Analysis

### คำถามที่ต้องตอบ
> "ตลาดมีสุขภาพดีจริงๆ ไหม หรือแค่ดีเพราะหุ้นไม่กี่ตัว?"

### Breadth Indicators
```
A) NYSE ADVANCE/DECLINE LINE
   - A/D Line ทำ new high = ตลาดกว้าง = ดี
   - Price ทำ new high แต่ A/D Line ไม่ทำ = DIVERGENCE = ระวัง

B) % STOCKS ABOVE 50DMA / 200DMA
   > 70% above 50DMA = healthy market
   50-70% = moderate, selective
   < 50% = narrow, only large caps pulling
   < 30% = capitulation zone (อาจ contrarian buy)

C) NEW HIGHS vs NEW LOWS (52-Week)
   NH/NL Ratio > 2:1 = bullish breadth
   More New Lows than New Highs = distribution

D) EQUAL-WEIGHT vs CAP-WEIGHT
   RSP (equal-weight S&P) vs SPY (cap-weight)
   RSP > SPY = broad participation
   SPY > RSP significantly = only mega-caps driving

E) SMALL CAP vs LARGE CAP (IWM vs SPY)
   IWM outperforms = risk-on, broad economy strong
   IWM underperforms = narrowing market, recession risk building
```

### Leadership Quality
```
- ดู RS Leaders: หุ้น RS > 90th percentile กำลังเพิ่มหรือลดลง?
- Breakout Success Rate: Phase 2-3 breakouts ผ่านหรือ fail?
- Sector Leadership: Cyclicals lead = expansion; Defensives lead = caution
- Growth vs Value: ดูว่า market อยู่ phase ไหนของ cycle
```

## STEP 6: Earnings Regime Analysis

### คำถามที่ต้องตอบ
> "กำไรบริษัทกำลังดีขึ้นหรือแย่ลง? นักวิเคราะห์ปรับตัวเลขยังไง?"

### Earnings Framework
```
A) EPS REVISION TREND (สำคัญมาก)
   - ตลาดไม่ react ต่อ EPS สูง/ต่ำ
   - ตลาด react ต่อ EPS relative to expectations
   - Earnings Revision Breadth: % บริษัทที่ revise ขึ้น vs ลง
   
   > 55% revising up = BULLISH earnings regime
   45-55% = neutral
   < 45% = bearish earnings regime

B) GUIDANCE SENTIMENT
   - Beat + Raise = หุ้น respond ดีที่สุด
   - Beat + Maintain = OK
   - Beat + Lower = "dead cat" → ระวัง
   - Miss + Lower = ขาย ไม่มีข้อยกเว้น
   
C) MARGIN TREND
   - Gross Margin expanding = pricing power ยังมี
   - Operating Leverage: Revenue +X% → EPS +2X% = positive leverage
   - Negative leverage: Revenue ไม่โต แต่ costs โต = squeeze

D) CAPEX TREND
   - Capex เพิ่มขึ้น = บริษัทเชื่อใน future growth
   - Capex ตัด = cost-cutting mode = earnings quality ลดลง
   - AI Capex cycle (2024-2027): Data center, chips = secular tailwind

E) EARNINGS SEASON PATTERN
   - 70% ตี EPS = ปกติ; > 75% = strong beat season
   - Revenue beat rate มีน้ำหนักมากกว่า EPS beat (harder to manipulate)
   - Forward guidance ดูมากกว่า historical beat
```

### Earnings Regime Classification
```
ACCELERATION:  YoY EPS growth accelerating, Revisions positive, Beats > 70%
EXPANSION:     YoY EPS positive, Revisions neutral, Beats normal
DECELERATION:  YoY EPS still positive but slowing, Revisions mixed
CONTRACTION:   YoY EPS negative, Revisions negative, Beats < 60%
TROUGH:        EPS deeply negative, Revisions maximally negative, Turning?
```

# SECTION 4: ADVANCED THINKING METHODS

## Method 1: First Derivative Thinking (Rate of Change)
```
ตลาดตอบสนองต่อ CHANGES ไม่ใช่ LEVELS

EXAMPLE:
- PMI ที่ 48 กำลังขึ้นจาก 44 = BULLISH (momentum turning)
- PMI ที่ 58 กำลังลงจาก 62 = BEARISH (deceleration)

- Inflation ที่ 5% กำลังลงจาก 8% = BULLISH (disinflation trade)
- Inflation ที่ 2.5% กำลังขึ้นจาก 1.5% = BEARISH (re-flation scare)

- Unemployment ที่ 5% กำลังลงจาก 7% = BULLISH (labor improving)
- Unemployment ที่ 3.5% กำลังขึ้น = BEARISH (recession signal)

PRACTICE: เสมอถามว่า "กำลัง better หรือ worse กว่าเดือนที่แล้ว?"
```

## Method 2: Second Derivative Thinking (Acceleration/Deceleration)
```
ดู Rate of change ของ Rate of change

EXAMPLE:
- EPS growth: -30% → -20% → -10% → +5%
  ยัง negative แต่ second derivative positive = ใกล้ bottom
  ตลาดมักขึ้น 6-9 เดือนก่อน EPS กลับเป็น positive

- Claims: rising แต่ slower each week = labor market softening แต่ stabilizing

- HY Spread: widening แต่ rate of widening ลดลง = stress peaking

APPLICATION:
- ใช้ second derivative เพื่อ detect inflection EARLY
- อย่ารอให้ direction เปลี่ยนก่อน — ดู momentum ของ direction แทน
```

## Method 3: Reflexivity (Soros)
```
Narrative → Price → Capital Allocation → Earnings → Narrative reinforced

EXAMPLE — AI NARRATIVE (2023-2025):
Step 1: Narrative "AI will change everything" → Tech stocks ขึ้น
Step 2: Tech stocks สูง → Capital flows to AI companies
Step 3: Capital flows → AI companies รายได้จริง
Step 4: Real revenues → Narrative confirmed → Stocks ขึ้นต่อ

REFLEXIVITY REVERSAL (เมื่อ cycle พัง):
Step 1: AI spend disappointment → narrative doubted
Step 2: Stocks ลง → Capital retreat
Step 3: Capital retreat → Less AI spend → Earnings miss
Step 4: Earnings miss → Narrative collapses → Further decline

WATCH FOR: ช่วงที่ narrative กำลัง reinforce vs กำลัง unravel
```

## Method 4: Cross-Asset Confirmation
```
ห้ามเชื่อ signal จาก asset class เดียว — ต้อง confirm cross-asset

BULLISH CONFIRMATION (3+ ต้องตรงกัน):
✅ Equities: Breadth ดี, A/D Line making new highs
✅ Credit: HY spread แคบลง, IG tight
✅ FX: USD อ่อนลง (EM/commodity benefitting)
✅ Rates: Real rates ลง หรือ yield curve steepening
✅ Commodities: Copper (Dr. Copper) ขึ้น = demand ดีจริง

BEARISH CONFIRMATION:
⚠️ Credit widening ก่อน equities ตก = leading warning
⚠️ DXY แข็งขึ้นแรงๆ = global tightening
⚠️ Copper ลง = real demand weakening
⚠️ 10Y yield ขึ้นเพราะ inflation ไม่ใช่ growth = bad yield
⚠️ A/D Line diverging from price = distribution
```

## Method 5: Positioning Analysis
```
OVERCROWDED + GOOD FUNDAMENTALS = VULNERABLE
UNDERPOSITIONED + BAD FUNDAMENTALS = PAIN TRADE POTENTIAL

Data Sources:
- CFTC Commitment of Traders (COT): futures positioning
- AAII Sentiment Survey: retail investor positioning
- Hedge Fund positioning via 13F filings
- Put/Call Ratio: options market sentiment
- Goldman Sachs Hedge Fund VIP list: crowded long ideas
- BofA Fund Manager Survey: institutional positioning

RULE: Crowded = vulnerability, even if thesis is correct
      Under-owned = potential pain trade if narrative shifts
```

## Method 6: Narrative Lifecycle
```
Phase 1: SKEPTICISM
- Few people believe the thesis
- Valuation still "normal" or cheap
- Smart money accumulating quietly
- Characteristic: Ignored by mainstream media

Phase 2: EARLY ADOPTION
- Thesis starts gaining traction
- Initial earnings confirmation
- RS starts outperforming
- Characteristic: Covered in niche publications

Phase 3: EARNINGS CONFIRMATION
- Numbers actually come in strong
- Mainstream starts covering
- Institutional buying accelerating
- Characteristic: "I should have bought this"

Phase 4: INSTITUTIONAL CROWDING
- Widely known thesis
- Everyone owns it or wants to
- Valuation premium well-established
- Characteristic: Cover stories, consensus bullish

Phase 5: OVER-OWNERSHIP
- At capacity — who else can buy?
- Any bad news causes outsized selloff
- Characteristic: "Too big to fail" mentality

Phase 6: SATURATION
- Revenue growth decelerating (even if still positive)
- Marginal buyers exhausted
- Smart money starts distributing
- Characteristic: "Growth still good but..." articles

Phase 7: DE-RATING
- Multiple compression even with decent earnings
- Narrative questioned
- Characteristic: Rapid institutional exit

APPLY TO: 
- AI narrative (currently Phase 3-4, 2025)
- Memory cycle (Phase 2-3, 2026)
- Space economy (Phase 1-2, 2026)
```

```
- USD (risk-on, dollar weakens)
- Defensive stocks (healthcare, utilities underperform)
- Short-duration bonds

HISTORICAL EXAMPLES:
- 1995-1999 (tech bull run)
- 2013-2014 (Fed taper period ended)
- H2 2023 (AI + soft landing)

DURATION: Usually 12-24 months, then tips to Late-cycle
EXIT SIGNAL: Inflation starts rising again OR credit starts widening
```

### Regime 2: REFLATION 🟢
```
Definition: Growth ↑, Inflation ↑ (but manageable), Liquidity adequate, Credit tight

TRIGGER: Usually comes from commodity boom or demand surge

WINNING ASSETS:
- Energy (oil, gas, coal)
- Materials (copper, steel, miners)
- Financials (banks: yield curve steepening)
- Industrials (capex boom)
- Small/Mid caps

LOSING ASSETS:
- Long-duration tech
- Consumer staples
- Gold (real rates rising)
- Bonds

DECISION TREE:
IF PMI rising AND yield curve steepening AND HY spreads tight
AND energy/commodity prices rising
→ Reflation regime → Overweight cyclicals, underweight growth
```

### Regime 3: EARLY-CYCLE RECOVERY 🟡🟢
```
Definition: Coming out of recession. Growth still negative but 2nd derivative positive.
            Earnings still bad but beating very low expectations.
            Liquidity maximum (Fed just started easing)

PARADOX: Best time to buy stocks, but hardest time psychologically
         Everyone is still traumatized from the downturn

WINNING ASSETS:
- Banks (yield curve uninverts, credit cycle starting)
- Consumer Discretionary
- Small caps (most beaten down, highest leverage to recovery)
- EM (dollar declining)
- Cyclicals broadly

LOSING ASSETS:
- Defensive (already worked, now underperform)
- Gold (fear premium collapsing)
- Bonds (rates starting to rise)

SIGNAL TO ENTER EARLY CYCLE:
- Claims peak + start falling
- ISM new orders bottom
- Credit conditions stop tightening (SLOOS)
- Yield curve uninverting / steepening
- Fed announces first cut
```

### Regime 4: LATE-CYCLE OVERHEATING ⚠️
```
Definition: Growth ↑ but slowing, Inflation ↑ persistent, Fed hiking, 
            Earnings good but decelerating, Credit tightening

CHARACTERISTICS:
- "Everything still looks great on the surface"
- But forward indicators deteriorating
- Credit conditions tightening = lag effect coming
- Yield curve inverting

WINNING ASSETS:
- Value stocks (not growth)
- Energy (sustained high commodity prices)
- Healthcare (defensive growth)
- Cash (wait for better entry)
- Short-dated bonds (avoid duration)

LOSING ASSETS:
- High multiple growth stocks (WACC rising kills valuations)
- Consumer discretionary (credit tightening → spending slows)
- Real estate (rate-sensitive)
- Long-duration bonds

DETECTION:
- Fed rate is above neutral (> 4%)
- Yield curve inverted (T10Y2Y < -0.5%)
- SLOOS net tightening > 30%
- EPS growth decelerating
- Claims starting to tick up
```

### Regime 5: STAGFLATION 🔴⚠️
```
Definition: Growth ↓ + Inflation ↑ = WORST COMBO for financial assets

CAUSES:
- Supply shock (oil embargo 1973, energy crisis 2022)
- War/geopolitical event (Ukraine 2022, Iran 2026)
- Trade war + tariffs
- Supply chain collapse

CHARACTERISTICS:
- Fed stuck: cannot cut (inflation too high) nor hike without destroying growth
- Earnings squeezed: cost pressure + demand weakness
- Bonds AND stocks both perform poorly (no safe haven in traditional 60/40)

WINNING ASSETS:
- Commodities (direct inflation hedge)
- Gold (stagflation historically great for gold)
- Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS)
- Energy stocks
- Real assets (infrastructure, land)
- Short positions

LOSING ASSETS:
- Growth stocks (double squeeze: valuation + earnings)
- Consumer Discretionary
- Long-duration bonds
- Most equities

MAY 2026 ASSESSMENT:
- Iran war → Energy shock → Core inflation re-accelerating
- Market has priced OUT rate cuts for 2026
- Some traders pricing rate HIKE by December
- This is classic Stagflation setup → Maximum caution on growth stocks
```

### Regime 6: RECESSION 🔴🔴
```
Definition: GDP negative 2 quarters, Unemployment rising, EPS collapsing,
            Credit stress, Earnings misses

CHARACTERISTICS:
- Consumer spending plunges
- Business investment halts
- Credit spreads blow out (HY > 7%)
- Fed cutting aggressively (but lagged)

WINNING ASSETS:
- Long-duration Treasuries (risk-free + price appreciation as yields fall)
- Gold (fear premium + dollar weakens)
- Healthcare (defensive)
- Consumer Staples (non-discretionary demand)
- Cash

LOSING ASSETS:
- Almost everything else
- Especially: High yield bonds, Cyclicals, Financials, Small caps

DETECTION (leading by 6-12 months):
- Yield curve inverted for 6+ months
- SLOOS net tightening > 40%
- ISM below 46 with new orders declining
- Claims rising consistently
- Housing starts collapsing
- IG spreads > 150bp AND widening
```

### Regime 7: LIQUIDITY MELT-UP 🟢🟢🟢 (Dangerous)
```
Definition: Central bank floods system with liquidity. Fundamentals don't matter much.
            Price driven by money supply, not earnings.

CHARACTERISTICS:
- "Don't fight the Fed/central banks"
- Junk goes up as much as quality
- High multiple = high gains
- Everything goes up: stocks, bonds, gold, crypto, real estate

EXAMPLES:
- QE1, QE2, QE3 (2009-2015)
- COVID QE (2020-2021)

WINNING: Everything, especially long-duration and speculative
LOSING: Short positions, cash (devalued)

DANGER SIGNAL:
- Ends violently when central bank pivots to QT/rate hikes
- Inflation signals the end
- Follow the central bank calendar, not the fundamentals
```

### Regime 8: CREDIT STRESS 🔴
```
Definition: Credit markets seizing. Not yet recession but risk of contagion.
            HY spread 5-7%, IG widening, lending standards max tight.

EXAMPLES:
- SVB collapse March 2023
- European debt crisis 2011-2012
- Repo market stress 2019

CHARACTERISTICS:
- Credit leads equity down
- Forced selling by levered players
- Correlation to 1 (everything falls together)
- Liquidity disappears

RESPONSE:
- Move to Treasuries + Gold + Cash
- Avoid anything with credit risk
- Watch for Fed/ECB emergency response
```

### Regime 9: DEFLATION SCARE 🟡
```
Definition: Fear of deflation. Growth slowing sharply. Inflation falling too fast.
            Japan 1990s as extreme example.

CHARACTERISTICS:
- Bonds rally strongly (yield falls)
- Central banks out of ammunition (near zero rates)
- QE becomes the only tool

WINNING: Long-duration Treasuries, Gold, Utilities
LOSING: Banks (net interest margin collapses), Energy, Cyclicals

RARE IN US but relevant for monitoring Japan/China/Europe signals
```

# SECTION 6: DECISION TREES

## Primary Decision Tree — Entry to Regime Classification

```
START: Gather all 6 factor scores

STEP 1: Check Liquidity Score
├── Score ≥ 4 → Liquidity supportive → Proceed to Step 2
└── Score ≤ 2 → Liquidity constraining → Risk of Credit Stress / Recession

STEP 2: Check Credit Score
├── HY Spread < 3.5% → Credit calm → Proceed to Step 3
├── HY Spread 3.5-5% → Caution → Half sizing, monitor closely
└── HY Spread > 5% → Stress → Defensive mode, check for Credit Stress regime

STEP 3: Check Growth Direction (First Derivative)
├── PMI rising → Growth accelerating → Bullish overlay
├── PMI flat → Growth stagnating → Neutral overlay
└── PMI falling → Growth decelerating → Bearish overlay

STEP 4: Check Inflation Direction
├── Inflation falling → Disinflation → Valuation-supportive
├── Inflation stable → Neutral
└── Inflation rising → Tightening pressure → Rate hike risk

STEP 5: Cross Liquidity + Growth + Inflation → Regime
├── Liquidity ↑ + Growth ↑ + Inflation ↓ = GOLDILOCKS
├── Liquidity ↑ + Growth ↑ + Inflation ↑ = REFLATION / LATE CYCLE
├── Liquidity ↑ + Growth ↓ = EARLY RECOVERY (if inflation also ↓)
├── Liquidity ↓ + Growth ↓ + Inflation ↑ = STAGFLATION
├── Liquidity ↓ + Growth ↓ + Inflation ↓ = RECESSION
└── Liquidity ↑↑ (CB printing) regardless of fundamentals = LIQUIDITY MELT-UP

STEP 6: Check Market Internals for Confirmation
├── Breadth confirms → Add conviction to regime call
└── Breadth contradicts → Reduce conviction, look deeper

STEP 7: Check Earnings Regime
├── Earnings accelerating → Regime reinforced
└── Earnings decelerating → Regime more fragile than it appears

FINAL: Output Regime + Conviction + Asset Allocation
```

```
- ระบุ data ที่สนับสนุน bearish case
- ระบุว่า bear case require อะไรเป็น true

"What is the mainstream/consensus view currently?"
- อะไรที่ตลาด price in แล้ว (ไม่มีประโยชน์ซ้ำอีก)
- Market positioning บ่งชี้อะไร

CORNER 4 — NON-CONSENSUS (Most Valuable)
"What is the market NOT pricing? What would surprise everyone?"
- Pain trade คืออะไร?
- อะไรที่ถ้าเกิดขึ้นจะทำให้ consensus scramble?
- Positioning data บ่งชี้ว่าใครจะถูก squeezed?

RESOLUTION:
- ชั่งน้ำหนัก 4 corners
- กำหนด Regime ที่ most likely
- กำหนด Highest-conviction positioning
- กำหนด Key Risk (สิ่งที่จะทำให้ call ผิด)
```

```
   - New signal emerges → add to framework, weight 50% until validated

OUTPUT: Weekly learning note in memory/macro_regime_log.md
```

## Regime Transition Detection Protocol
```
WHEN TO DECLARE REGIME CHANGE:

STRONG SIGNAL (declare immediately):
- Macro Scorecard shifts by > 8 points
- 4+ factors change direction simultaneously
- Central bank makes emergency policy pivot

MEDIUM SIGNAL (declare on confirmation):
- 3 factors change direction
- Key cross-asset confirms (e.g., credit + equity breadth both diverging)
- 2nd derivative turns negative for 3 consecutive weeks

WEAK SIGNAL (note, monitor, do not change):
- 1-2 factors showing mixed signals
- A/D line diverging but credit still calm
- One earnings miss without broader pattern

RULE: Regime change is declared when the weight of evidence shifts,
      not when one number crosses a line.
```
