# SECTION A: UNIVERSAL LAWS
## "สิ่งที่เป็นจริงทุก cycle ทุก regime"

### Law 1: Credit Leads Equity
```
HY spread widen ก่อน equity ตก เสมอ
→ ถ้า HY กำลัง widen แต่ equity ยังขึ้น → เชื่อ credit ไม่ใช่ equity
→ HY กลับมา calm → equity ขึ้นต่อได้

EMPIRICAL: ก่อน 2008: HY widened H1 2007, equity peaked Oct 2007
           ก่อน 2020 COVID: HY สัญญาณ Feb 14, equity peaked Feb 19
           2022: HY led equity down ทุก wave
```

### Law 2: Liquidity First, Fundamentals Second
```
Michael Howell: "Global Liquidity leads earnings by 6-9 months"

ในช่วง QE:  fundamentals bad แต่ liquidity เยอะ → asset prices ขึ้น
ในช่วง QT:  fundamentals OK แต่ liquidity ลด → asset prices กด

→ อย่าต่อสู้กับ CB เมื่อ QT กำลังเกิด
→ อย่าถือ cash มากเกินเมื่อ Fed เปิด QE
```

### Law 3: Rate of Change > Absolute Level
```
"Markets move on surprises vs. expectations, not on absolute values"

PMI 48 ↑ จาก 44 → Bullish (beat expectation)
PMI 54 ↓ จาก 58 → Bearish (miss expectation)

Inflation 3% ↓ จาก 6% → Goldilocks narrative
Inflation 3% ↑ จาก 1.5% → Tightening narrative

→ ก่อนอ่านตัวเลขใดๆ ถามว่า "เทียบกับเมื่อเดือนที่แล้วเป็นยังไง?"
```

### Law 4: Consensus is Already Priced
```
"If everyone knows it, it's already in the price"

→ ข่าวดีที่ทุกคนรู้ = ราคาไม่ขึ้นเพิ่ม (sell the news)
→ ข่าวร้ายที่ทุกคนกลัว = ราคาลงแล้ว (buy the fear, selectively)
→ เสมอถามว่า "นี่ consensus view ไหม? ถ้าใช่ — ราคา price in ไปแล้วเท่าไหร่?"
```

### Law 5: Yield Curve เป็น Oracle
```
Yield Curve inversion (10Y < 2Y) → recession follows ใน 12-24 เดือน เสมอ
False negative: ไม่เคยมี (ทุก inversion ≥ 6 เดือน → recession)
False positive: 1967 (near miss แต่ไม่ถึง recession)

→ Curve inverted > 6 เดือน = ต้องเตรียม defensives
→ Curve uninverting (steepening) = early recovery signal
```

### Law 6: Gold = Hedge ต่อ Uncertainty ไม่ใช่แค่ Inflation
```
Gold ขึ้นในสภาพ:
  - Real rates ลด (แม้ไม่มี inflation)
  - Credit stress / banking crisis
  - Geopolitical uncertainty
  - CB diversifying away from USD
  - Fiscal debt concerns (US deficit expansion)

Gold ลงในสภาพ:
  - Real rates สูงขึ้น (Volcker 1981, 2022-2023)
  - Risk appetite สูง (Goldilocks equity bull)
  - DXY แข็งขึ้น
```

### Law 7: Dollar Smile Theory
```
USD แข็งใน 2 กรณี (Smile):
  LEFT SIDE: US economy outperforms (capital flows in)
  RIGHT SIDE: Global risk-off (flight to safety)

USD อ่อนใน 1 กรณี (Bottom of smile):
  MIDDLE: Global synchronous growth (EM/commodity ดึงทุนออก)

→ ถ้า USD แข็งแบบ "right side" → อย่าซื้อ EM, commodity
→ ถ้า USD อ่อนเพราะ global growth → EM outperforms
```

# SECTION B: PER-REGIME PLAYBOOKS
## "สิ่งที่ทำแล้วได้ผลใน regime นั้น"

### GOLDILOCKS Playbook
```
DEFINITION: Growth ↑, Inflation ↓, Liquidity adequate, Credit tight (good)

WHAT WORKS:
1. GROWTH STOCKS / HIGH MULTIPLE TECH
   → Long duration assets benefit from low discount rate
   → Nasdaq typically outperforms S&P by 5-15% in this regime

2. SMALL CAPS (IWM)
   → Economy broad = small cap participates
   → Historically +25-40% from regime onset to peak

3. EM EQUITIES
   → Dollar weakens → EM capital inflows
   → Best: EM with tech (Korea, Taiwan) or commodity (Brazil)

4. IG CORPORATE BONDS
   → Tight credit, falling yields = price appreciation
   → Avoid: Treasuries (yields rising from growth)

5. CYCLICAL GROWTH (Software, Semis, E-Commerce)
   → Revenue accelerating into strong consumer demand

WHAT DOESN'T WORK:
- Gold (real rates rising)
- Defensive stocks (utilities, staples underperform)
- Short USD
- Long Treasuries (duration hurts as yields rise)

TIMING:
- Entry: PMI bottoms AND inflation peaks AND first rate cut
- Exit: Inflation re-accelerates OR yield curve flattens significantly

DURATION: Typically 12-24 months
```

### STAGFLATION Playbook
```
DEFINITION: Growth ↓ + Inflation ↑ = Fed stuck, no good choices

WHAT WORKS:
1. COMMODITIES (direct)
   → Physical gold, oil, copper, agricultural
   → Commodity producers: energy companies, miners

2. GOLD SPECIFICALLY
   → Stagflation historically best environment for gold
   → 1973-1974: Gold +72%, S&P -43%
   → 2022: Gold held while S&P -20%, Tech -35%

3. TIPS (Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities)
   → Principal adjusts with CPI
   → Better than nominal bonds in stagflation

4. SHORT DURATION / FLOATING RATE
   → Avoid long-duration bonds (inflation erodes value)
   → Leveraged loans, floating rate notes

5. VALUE STOCKS (not growth)
   → Companies with real assets, pricing power
   → Energy majors, utilities with inflation pass-through

6. CASH (wait for better entry)
   → Stagflation is hardest to trade
   → Capital preservation > alpha generation

WHAT DOESN'T WORK:
- Long-duration tech (WACC rises, terminal value crushed)
- Consumer discretionary (discretionary spend cut first)
- Real estate (rate-sensitive)
- 60/40 portfolio (both stocks AND bonds suffer)
- EM tech (dollar strengthens, EM tech punished doubly)

TIMING:
- Entry to defensive: When inflation re-accelerates after appearing to peak
- Look for: Energy shock, wage-price spiral signal, Fed hands-tied language
- Exit defensive: Demand destruction brings inflation down OR recession triggers Fed to cut

CURRENT STATUS (May 2026):
- Iran war → energy supply shock → inflation re-accelerating
- Market priced OUT all cuts — some pricing hike
- This is classic stagflation onset pattern
- Action: Overweight energy, gold, cash. Avoid high-multiple growth.
```

### EARLY RECOVERY Playbook
```
DEFINITION: Worst economically, best for buying

PSYCHOLOGICAL PARADOX: "It's always darkest before the dawn"
   → Hardest time to buy because sentiment maximum bearish
   → Best time to buy because everyone already sold

WHAT WORKS:
1. BANKS / FINANCIALS
   → Yield curve uninverting → net interest margin expands
   → Loan losses peak → credit cycle turns
   → Most hated sector at bottom = biggest recovery

2. SMALL CAPS
   → Most beaten down in recession
   → Highest operating leverage to recovery
   → IWM historically +40-80% from trough in 12-18 months

3. CONSUMER DISCRETIONARY
   → Pent-up demand releases
   → Fed cuts remove affordability burden

4. EM EQUITIES
   → Dollar weakens as Fed cuts → EM gets relief
   → China particularly responds to global demand recovery

5. HIGH YIELD BONDS
   → Massive discount from peak spread → compression trade
   → Risk: Be sure recession is ending, not deepening

SIGNALS TO ENTER:
- Initial Claims PEAK + turn lower for 3+ consecutive weeks
- ISM new orders bottom (leading by 2-3 months)
- Credit SLOOS stops tightening
- Fed signals first cut (or cut already happened)
- Market makes "higher low" on technical basis

TIMING IS EVERYTHING HERE:
- Too early (during recession) = value trap
- Correct (at economic bottom) = 3-5x return potential
- Too late (after consensus knows it) = normal return
```

### CREDIT STRESS Playbook
```
DEFINITION: HY spreading, credit markets seizing, not yet full recession

IMMEDIATE ACTION:
1. MOVE TO QUALITY
   → Sell HY bonds, junk debt, leveraged companies
   → Rotate to IG, Treasuries, cash
   
2. AVOID LEVERED BALANCE SHEETS
   → High debt = forced selling under credit stress
   → PE-backed = most vulnerable (LBO debt + refinancing risk)

3. WATCH FOR FED RESPONSE
   → Fed emergency action is the "buy signal"
   → 2008: TARP + QE → buy
   → 2020: $2T QE → buy
   → 2023 SVB: Bank Term Lending → stabilization

4. GOLD AND TREASURIES
   → Flight to safety = government bonds + gold bid
   → Not: Gold miners (credit stress = forced selling)

5. WATCH THE WATCH LIST:
   → Which sector has most stress? (usually the overleveraged one)
   → Commercial real estate? (2023-2024)
   → EM sovereign debt? (2022 Sri Lanka, Pakistan)
   → US regional banks? (2023 SVB)
```

# SECTION C: TIMING RULES
## "เมื่อไหร่ที่ต้องเปลี่ยน regime call"

### Regime Change Protocol
```
DECLARE CHANGE IMMEDIATELY when:
  ✅ Macro Scorecard shifts > 8 pts in one week
  ✅ 4+ factors simultaneously change direction
  ✅ CB makes emergency policy pivot (unscheduled meeting)
  ✅ HY spreads spike > 1.5% in 5 trading days
  ✅ Yield curve morphology changes drastically (e.g., inversion depth changes 50bp)

DECLARE CHANGE ON CONFIRMATION when:
  ✅ 3 factors change direction
  ✅ Credit + equity breadth both confirm (2 asset class minimum)
  ✅ Second derivative turns for 3 consecutive weeks

WATCH BUT DON'T CHANGE when:
  ⚠️ Only 1-2 factors show mixed signals
  ⚠️ A/D line diverges but credit is calm
  ⚠️ One earnings miss without broader pattern
  ⚠️ VIX spike but HY calm (spike = fear, not stress)

NEVER CHANGE BECAUSE:
  ❌ CIO prefers a different regime
  ❌ CNBC is talking about it
  ❌ One month of data reverses a 6-month trend
```

### Crowded Trade Warnings
```
PRINCIPLE: Crowded trade = maximum vulnerability
           Even if thesis is right, overcrowded = price already discounted future

HOW TO DETECT CROWDING:
  - Goldman Sachs Hedge Fund VIP list (top 10 positions of 700+ HFs)
  - CFTC COT data: max net long in futures
  - BofA Fund Manager Survey: "most crowded trade"
  - Put/Call ratio: extreme low put = complacency
  - Short interest collapse: no bears left = no fuel for short squeeze

HISTORICALLY CROWDED TRADES THAT BLEW UP:
  - Long Tech 2000 (everyone owned it → NASDAQ -80%)
  - Short VIX 2018 (VIX ETN implosion, Feb 5 "Volmageddon")
  - Long ARK Innovation 2021 (crowded speculative → -75%)
  - Long China 2020-2021 (regulatory risk underpriced)
  - Short bonds 2022 → long story, but positioning drove exaggerated moves

CURRENT POTENTIAL CROWDING (May 2026):
  - AI / Hyperscaler names (NVDA, MSFT, META) — widely held
  - Monitor: hedge fund concentration in AI names
  - If narrative shifts (AI ROI disappointment) → violent exit
```

### Pain Trade Identification
```
PAIN TRADE = move that hurts maximum number of people most

Identify by asking:
1. "What is consensus currently positioned for?"
2. "What would force them to cover/exit?"
3. "What would cause maximum surprise?"

May 2026 Pain Trades:
  Pain Trade A: Fed HIKES instead of cuts
  → Consensus: 0 hikes, maybe 1 cut eventually
  → If inflation re-accelerates → hike priced in → growth/tech hammered
  
  Pain Trade B: Iran ceasefire suddenly
  → Consensus: Iran conflict ongoing, oil elevated
  → Ceasefire → oil crashes → energy stocks -20%, inflation disappears
  → Fed cuts rapidly → growth stocks surge → short sellers crushed
  
  Pain Trade C: China stimulus surprise
  → EM and commodities not positioned for this
  → Large upside catch-up in copper, iron ore, EM equities
```
